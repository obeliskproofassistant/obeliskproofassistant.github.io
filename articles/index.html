Ideal models of MKTD1 and MKTD2

Two short articles, each stating the mathematical object that one of Obelisk's formal systems is meant to implement, and listing where the SML implementation departs from it. Both ideal models are formalised in Lean 4.

MKTD1: the language and calculus (PDF)

A classical sequent calculus for Morse–Kelley class theory with universes. It covers the sort-indexed expressions with quotation ⟨e⟩ and antiquotation ⟦u⟧, and reflection through Holds, Omnis and Credo.

Lean: the expression family is written once for every metalevel. The calculus with Omnis and Credo is proved sound for Holds read as validity, assuming that 𝓕 has a model. The paper also explains why restricting comprehension to object-level formulas makes the semantics of Holds well founded, and it proves Russell's theorem.

MKTD2: the language and calculus (PDF)

A dependent type theory with an impredicative Prop and universes Typei, made classical by postulates.

Lean: a de Bruijn calculus with transport along equality. A bidirectional checker is proved sound: whatever it accepts is derivable, for every fuel bound. Regression tests cover the two soundness bugs fixed in the SML kernel, and symmetry of equality is derived instead of postulated.

Sources: the LaTeX files are in set_theory_prover/docs/models/ and the Lean files in set_theory_prover/formal/lean/ (repository).
