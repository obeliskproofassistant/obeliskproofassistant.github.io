6.02 Theorem Löb's Theorem
comment
Formalized Henkin modal provability principle.
\(\forall (\phi : \mathsf{Fm})\; (\mathcal{Q}, [] \vdash ((\mathcal{T}, [] \vdash ((\mathcal{T}, [] \vdash \phi) \to \phi)) \to (\mathcal{T}, [] \vdash \phi)))\)
\(\forall \phi \; (\mathcal{Q}, [] \vdash ((\mathcal{T}, [] \vdash ((\mathcal{T}, [] \vdash \phi) \to \phi)) \to (\mathcal{T}, [] \vdash \phi)))\)

6.03 Theorem Gödel's Second Incompleteness Theorem
comment
Consistency cannot be proved within the system itself.
\(\mathcal{Q}, [] \vdash (\neg(\mathcal{T}, [] \vdash \bot) \to \neg(\mathcal{T}, [] \vdash \neg(\mathcal{T}, [] \vdash \bot)))\)
\(\mathcal{Q}, [] \vdash (\neg(\mathcal{T}, [] \vdash \bot) \to \neg(\mathcal{T}, [] \vdash \neg(\mathcal{T}, [] \vdash \bot)))\)
 \(\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{Q} : \mathsf{Theory}\)\(\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{Q}\) Weakly Verified (Lit)
