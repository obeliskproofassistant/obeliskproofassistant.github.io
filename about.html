Greetings

This site contains a demonstration of the development of a proof assistant, as well as various related links, texts, presentations, and materials about the foundations of mathematics.

The project aims to achieve three results:

  • a new proof assistant with significantly reduced learning curve and bidirectional communication with mathematical community: it doesn't require a user to learn additional information about term normalisation, foundations, tactics, and many others notions, which are typically unusual for a mathematician to know;
  • a new programming language with certain nice properties(e.g.: better polymorphism) via adding additional abstract layer on compiler of the existing project CakeML;
  • "refactored", but still traditional class-theoretic foundations of mathematics.

Theory at a Glance: MKTD (Morse–Kelley + Tarski + Dunaev)

The Obelisk Proof Assistant formalizes Morse–Kelley class theory with Tarski–Grothendieck universes and Dunaev foundations (MKTD).

Dimension Specification in Obelisk (MKTD) Comparison / Notes
Formal Theory MKTD (Morse–Kelley + Tarski + Dunaev) Impredicative class comprehension over proper classes.
Comprehension Bounds {x | φ(x)}: Rule 3.01 admits formulas φ quantifying over all classes (including proper classes). Strictly stronger than NBG, which restricts φ to quantify only over sets (Δ0-comprehension).
Consistency Strength Equiconsistent with ZFC + ∃ κ (strongly inaccessible cardinal). Proves Con(ZFC) and Con(NBG). Stronger than second-order arithmetic.
Class Pairing Dunaev Tagged Ordered Pairs: (A, B)D ≡ ({1} × A) ∪ ({2} × B). Sound for proper classes (Kuratowski pairs {{a},{a,b}} collapse on proper classes).
Two Assistant Variants MKTD1 (Syntactic Sequent Derivation) co-exists with MKTD2 (Computational Propositions-as-Types). MKTD1 checks derivation trees over formula-expression data (Truth.Expression ASTs) with explicit side conditions; MKTD2 checks proof-as-program λ-terms inhabiting proposition-types via bidirectional typing and Normalization-by-Evaluation (NbE).

Kernel Metatheory & Precise Soundness Guarantee

The kernel guarantee “Can a false statement pass? Never” is made formally precise by the following metatheorems:

  • Relative Soundness: Assuming consistency of the MKTD axioms, there is no closed proof term e such that ∅ ⊢ e : ⊥. Every constructor in the kernel is checked; untrusted placeholders (PCheat_prf) are strictly rejected.
  • Subject Reduction: If Γ ⊢ e : T and e →βδ e', then Γ ⊢ e' : T. Typing is invariant under term evaluation and definition unrolling.
  • Decidability of Definitional (α/β/δ) Equality: Term normalization is deterministic and terminating for all definitions registered in the opaque KernelDefinitions store.
  • Conservativity of Definitions: All new definitions are verified by KernelDefinitions :> KERNEL_DEFINITIONS to contain no free RHS variables, no circularity, and matching parameters, guaranteeing conservative extension.
  • Trusted Computing Base (TCB): Strictly confined to the Standard ML checker (newchecker.ML / nbg_compiler.ML), primitive rules (Modus Ponens, Generalisation), propositional axioms (A01–A13), and the primitive set-theoretic axioms (Extensionality, Heredity, Regularity, Exponent, Global Choice, and Universes).

Propositions-as-Types Alongside Classical Axioms

In the higher-order variant (MKTD2 / Rocq), propositions are treated as types (implications as function spaces P → Q, universal quantifiers as dependent products Π(x:X), P(x)):

  • Postulated Axioms as Constant Proof Constructors: Classical axioms such as Excluded Middle (A12) and Global Choice appear in proof terms as uninterpreted constants (axiom witnesses) in the signature Δ.
  • Classical Inhabitation: Inhabiting a proposition P means exhibiting a well-typed term Δ; Γ ⊢ e : P constructed from constructive λ-calculus rules and these classical axiom constants.
  • Proof Irrelevance: Once a proof term is successfully typed by the kernel, any two proofs of the same proposition P are epistemically equivalent truth certificates.
