Obelisk Proof Assistant

Greetings

This site contains a demonstration of the development of a proof assistant, as well as various related links, texts, presentations, and materials about the foundations of mathematics.

The project aims to achieve three results:

  • a new proof assistant,
  • a new programming language with certain nice properties,
  • "refactored" classical class-theoretic foundations of mathematics.
