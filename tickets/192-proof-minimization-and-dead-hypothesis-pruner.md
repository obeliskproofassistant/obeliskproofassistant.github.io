## 1. Description & Technical Rationale
Constructive proofs generated by tactics or manual derivation often accumulate unused hypotheses (`Gamma`) or redundant intermediate weakening steps. For instance, in sequent calculus derivations, hypothesis contexts can retain propositions that are never invoked in subsequent elimination rules.

This ticket specifies a proof term optimizer that prunes unused antecedents from derivation trees, yielding minimal proof terms that compile faster in both CakeML and Poly/ML.

---

## 2. Technical Specification & Defense Invariants
1. **Liveness Analysis for Hypotheses:**
   - Traverse the SML proof AST (`Prf` constructors: `PIdent`, `PModusPonens`, `PForallElim`, etc.).
   - Compute the exact set of referenced hypothesis variables `VarSet`.
   - Remove any hypothesis `h in Gamma` where `h not in VarSet`.
2. **Soundness Invariant:**
   - Minimization must never alter the proven conclusion formula `phi`.
   - The pruned term must strictly typecheck under the unmodified LCF microkernel.
3. **Web Presentation:**
   - Display both "Full Tactic Proof" and "Optimized Minimal Proof" in `proofs/item-*.html` and `proofs.html`.
