Why Formulas ≠ Proofs
1. Formula = Type (NBGCompiler.Type):
Writing formula F = Set({x | x in x -> Bot}); defines a syntactically well-formed Type (even though it is mathematically false / uninhabited). Defining a formula never proves anything!

2. Proof = Term (NBGCompiler.Expr):
To prove F, you must construct a term e from kernel primitives whose inferred type equals F. For false MK/NBG formulas, the compiler deterministically rejects every candidate term.
