Reflected Metatheory Specification

When moving from object-theory to metatheory, syntactic side conditions become first-class semantic statements, accompanied by meta-metatheoretic conditions.

The Metatheory Invariant

The key epistemic insight of the Obelisk Proof Assistant is that the metatheory is the same Morse–Kelley (MK) theory:

  • Reflected Side Conditions: What appears as a "syntactic side condition" on the manual page is reflected in the Semantic column here. It is not an axiom or theorem, but an ordinary formula whose truth value is checkable or provable.
  • Constant Identifiers: What was an object variable \(x\) becomes an unspecified constant identifier \(v_x : \mathsf{Variable}\) in the metatheory. We assume a standard set-theoretic encoding of the syntax language.
  • Side-Side Conditions: The "Syntax" column at this level represents meta-metatheoretic requirements (typing, context finiteness, structural induction termination).
