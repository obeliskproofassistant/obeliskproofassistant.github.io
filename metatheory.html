Reflected Metatheory Specification

When moving from object-theory to metatheory, syntactic side conditions become first-class semantic statements, accompanied by meta-metatheoretic conditions.

💡
Epistemic Soundness & Level Disparity Invariant: Notation is fragile—always consult typetheory.ML for semantics, never surface syntax! We distinguish between: (1) Meaning in Model inside NBG (pure set/class universe: \( orall X\), \( orall (x \in V)\), \(\mathrm{isSet}(x)\), strictly no category errors like \(\in \mathrm{Theory}\) or \(\in \mathrm{Class}\)), and (2) Checkable or Executable Code in CakeML & Poly/ML (Standard ML AST expressions: Class_Expre, Fm_Expre, Theory_Expre). Off-by-One Level Rule: Standard ML cannot have a type of all classes or sets (type Class = Nonstored.t); it stores expressions of classes (Class_Expre). Decidable side conditions are directly executable and testable boolean functions in CakeML and Poly/ML.

The Recursive Side Condition Tower & Metatheory Invariant

The key epistemic insight of the Obelisk Proof Assistant is that the same Morse–Kelley (MK) theory serves as both object theory and metatheory:

  • Recursive Tower of Formulas: Side conditions can have side-side conditions, side-side conditions can have side-side-side conditions, etc. Formulas are stored as formulas regardless of their metalevel: Level 0 (host axioms/theorems), Level 1 (metatheoretic side condition formula), Level 2 (metametatheoretic side-side condition formula), and Level 3 (ambient well-formedness context).
  • Decidability as Formal Metatheorems: If it is claimed that a side condition formula is decidable, then that decidability is itself established as a formal theorem in the metatheory with a constructive kernel witness algorithm.
  • Constants Representing Variables: What was an object variable \(x\) becomes an unspecified constant identifier \( \mathbf{v}_x \in \mathsf{Variable} \) in the metatheory. Constants representing variables are rendered distinctly in upright pink (v_x, v_C), preventing confusion with italic blue object variables.
  • Formulas Without Superfluous Typing Suffixes: A side condition like \( \mathrm{NFV}(\mathbf{v}_C, \phi) = \mathrm{True} \) is a genuine mathematical formula; it does not take a redundant : Bool or ∈ Bool suffix.
